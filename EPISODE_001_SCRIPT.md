People called the Coast Guard to report this vessel sinking. Its crew was doing it on purpose.

The stern dropped into the ocean. The bow climbed toward the sky.

And inside, there was a sink mounted sideways.

Because those walls were about to become the floor.

The sideways sink finally made sense.

And that was when the scientists could start work.

An ordinary ship rolls and pitches with the waves, shaking the instruments trying to measure them.

FLIP stretched deep below the surface, making it much more stable.

Scientists could study ocean sounds and currents while their laboratory barely moved.

When they finished, compressed air pushed the water out of the tanks, and FLIP lay back down.

So the sinking ship people reported was a laboratory getting ready for work.
