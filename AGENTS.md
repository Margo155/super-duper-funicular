# Oddplot Long 001 — production instructions

This is a handoff for a COMPLETE ten-minute horizontal film, The City They Had to Lift. Authoring this package is not the same task as rendering the film. When asked to produce the package, follow these instructions.

- Read README_RU.md, PROJECT_BRIEF.md, SCRIPT_EN_RU.md, STORYBOARD_60_SHOTS.md, PRODUCTION_PLAN.md, CONTINUITY_BIBLE.md, HISTORICAL_FACTS_AND_SOURCES.md, AUDIO_AND_CAPTIONS.md and QA_CHECKLIST.md.
- Inspect assets/hero_reference.png and the approved V3 reference MP4/PNGs. Reuse an existing editable rig and voice settings when available. The reference video is not an editable scene.
- Keep Short 001 (FLIP), Short 002 (penny) and their source intact. Work in a separate long_001_chicago folder. This order does not cancel either Short.
- Produce a 1920x1080, 16:9, 30 fps, exactly 600.000-second film: 18000 video frames. Do not stretch a portrait clip into a landscape film.
- Use audio/VOICEOVER_EN.txt verbatim: one English narrator, 1310 measured words. Russian is the owner's working language. The Russian translation is not a second voiceover track.
- The overall 600-second timeline is fixed; scene/shot boundaries are provisional until the actual voice is generated. Allocate the remaining time to written physical actions and sound beats, not frozen pictures or empty padding.
- Build actual economical volumetric sets with flat articulated 2D characters, lighting, contact shadows, motivated camera translation and parallax. Preserve the approved hero's head/body identity.
- Shoes stay attached during ordinary motion. The mud gag in shots S004–S006 explicitly removes/retrieves one shoe; it is an intentional planned exception and must show a coherent foot, loose shoe, and reattachment. It is not permission for accidental floating legs elsewhere.
- The homeowner, foreman and their actions are fictional. Historical claims must match the source map. Do not invent dialogue for Chesbrough or Pullman. The historical Lake Street insert is separate from the fictional house.
- Render in short resumable shots/chunks. Checkpoint scene files, per-shot clips, progress and commands. A failure in one shot must not require restarting 18000 frames.
- Test available render/edit/TTS tools and run a short local calibration clip as an implementation step. If it works, continue to the FULL film without asking to approve routine reversible steps. Do not stop after a storyboard, animatic or opening sample and call the order complete.
- No paid service without an agreed budget. No unauthorised voice cloning. Reuse an available authorised voice ID, or choose a suitable already available English voice and document it.
- Align final English captions to the actual new narration. Draft SRT/JSON are provisional. All on-screen labels should be English.
- Run the supplied video validator, build review sheets covering all 18000 frames, and inspect actual motion/audio. Technical checks do not prove rig continuity or historical correctness.
- Deliver the full narrated MP4, clean voice track, final SRT, editable source/assets, 60 control frames, all-frame review sheets, per-shot render progress, QA evidence and filled RENDER_NOTES.md. Preserve useful work and report specific blockers honestly.
- Publishing or uploading to a public channel is outside this task.
