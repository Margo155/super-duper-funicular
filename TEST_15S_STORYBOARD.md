All times are provisional editing targets. Make the voice first, then adjust
the cuts inside a 15-second / 450-frame output. The English narration is locked.
This is a staged animation of documented technology, not historical footage.

## Required technical checks visible in the test

- The mascot is a flat 2D element in a real 3D scene.
- Camera movement changes spatial relationships and reveals parallax.
- The light direction is consistent across face, deck, railings and props.
- Shoes stay on their assigned deck/floor plane, including the early hull tilt.
- The silhouette, eyes and hair match assets/hero_reference.png across shots.
- Narration is audible and matches the supplied English wording.
- Captions follow the actual audio; no subtitles extend past 15.00 seconds.

## Motion and pacing

Use motivated smooth moves and two or three clean edits/dissolves. There is no
need for constant shake, a large explosion, an intro logo, a subscription prompt
or the entire 90-degree flip inside this test. A small controlled early tilt
preserves the full episode's later reveal. Preserve useful source geometry for
the subsequent upright configuration.
